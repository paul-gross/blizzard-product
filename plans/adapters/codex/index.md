# Plan — `epic:adapters`, Codex slice

Codex is the third harness behind the adapter seam. It follows the same contract OpenCode establishes, and it waits for
OpenCode deliberately: only after two occupants have forced every Claude-shaped assumption into the binding that owns it
is the seam honest enough for a third to arrive as a binding and nothing more — a new adapter under harness selection
that already exists, not a new round of platform change.

The slice's requirements are not yet written. They follow the shape the [OpenCode slice](../opencode/index.md) sets:
prove the real CLI, make a usable worker, reach conversation parity, and harden it for unattended work.
