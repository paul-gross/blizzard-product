with the grid of epic slices that carried it.

## `milestone:centralized-hub` — run the fleet from anywhere

The hub stopped living where the operator sits: one hosted hub off the machine, runners signing in to it from wherever
they run, and a board and answer loop safe to reach from any device. Away from the desk no longer means away from the
fleet.

| Epic              | Slice delivered     | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `epic:security`   | runner-auth slice   | Runners sign in with hub-minted per-runner tokens; fleet writes answer to an authenticated router (#85 series). Worker-lockdown slice is in the [epic registry](./epics.md).                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `epic:board`      | remote (auth) slice | Provider login, viewer and operator roles, a capability-aware UI, runner single sign-on (#89). Its reach half was extracted to `epic:pwa`. Built from its [plan](./delivered/board/remote/index.md).                                                                                                                                                             |
| `epic:ask-answer` | remote slice        | The closed answer loop: an answer from any device shows its delivery and the agent back at work, and a beaten answerer is told who won (#165). | `epic:hub`        | remote slice        | The hub stands off-machine as a hosted deployment every runner reaches over the network. The plan's multi-runner proof and staged outage drill were retired by operator call: arbitration never assumed a runner count, and the reconnect-and-flush contract is exercised in production by every hub redeploy. Built from its [plan](./delivered/hub/remote.md). |

## `milestone:homeostasis` — a fleet that keeps its own house

The fleet took over its own vitals. Work can be born inside the hub rather than only at the forge, so a finding becomes
a filed item instead of a paragraph; every conversation from every runner reaches one store, and what the fleet does is
counted from it; gardening routines sweep the codebase along declared axes and turn decay into proposals the operator
accepts into ordinary work; mutation runs prove the backend suites can fail; the queue holds a chunk until the one it
stands on is done; and the fleet proposes product directions of its own. The `epic:cost` attribution slice the milestone
once named was retired unbuilt.

## `milestone:observability` — the fleet, in instruments you already trust (partial)

A fleet that works through the night produces evidence about itself and hands it over as files to any warehouse or
analytics tool, and as traces to any observability backend. Two exits for two kinds of question: records for the
quarter, traces for the night that mattered.

| Epic           | Slice delivered | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `epic:tracing` | fleet-spans     | Runner, hub and gate steps tell themselves as traces to an OTLP-speaking backend through a hub sweep. Each step is one root span with child spans for queue waits, gate outcomes, asks, hub executions, and invocations. Trace and span ids are pinned by test vectors. Configured via `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT`; persisted cursor, settle window, lag cap, and failure events; CLI `status` and `replay` commands for non-live access. Example two-backend collector config ships alongside. Built from its [plan](./delivered/tracing/fleet-spans/index.md).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `epic:tracing` | runner-spans    | Each runner tells every lease it closed into the trace of the step it ran, under the step identity the hub derives, with no message between the daemons. A `worker` span covers the lease; beneath it sit one GenAI-convention `invoke_agent` span per harness invocation with model, tokens and cost, spans for ask and pause parks, overload backoffs and takeovers, and events for checks, nudges and context samples. A runner sweep on its own thread, enabled by `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT`, never blocks a tick; `blizzard runner traces status` and `replay` mirror the hub's. Claude Code and OpenCode report the same shape, proven end to end under both mock harnesses (#772). Built from its [plan](./delivered/tracing/runner-spans/index.md).                                                                                                                                         |
| `epic:tracing` | platform-spans  | The agent's calls nest inside the step that made them. The hub and runner trace each API request, store query and outbound call through a redacting batch processor, keeping every call made inside a step and sampling roots at `platform_sample_ratio`; `heartbeat` is never traced. Each worker invocation carries the step's `traceparent`, worker CLI commands become spans without the OpenTelemetry SDK, and the runner's `/v1/traces` receiver checks them against the lease, stamps the caller and forwards them. The runner's per-step hub calls and the hub's `run:` steps parent on their steps, operator CLI commands trace to the configured endpoint, and `[tracing] worker_programs` opts other worker programs into the receiver. Proven end to end against `blizzard-mock`, with arrival order documented (#763). Built from its [plan](./delivered/tracing/platform-spans/index.md). |
