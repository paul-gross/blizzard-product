Sheet 3 — the docket, and the one decision that is a person's

Batched, not per-run

Proposals attach to their routine and are read across runs. That is what lets a run go end to end with nobody in it: the decision is available afterward, and it batches. The filters are state and class, because those are the two axes a person actually triages along.

Two verbs, and both leave a record

Pass is not a dismiss. It records that the proposal was considered and declined, with the reason, so a later cross-reference does not raise it again as new — and so “does anyone ever want this class of response?” becomes a query.

Accept is where the link is made

The body is prefilled and fully editable — paste in something drafted elsewhere if you like. What cannot move outside blizzard is the act: the item is created here so it carries the link to the proposal, through it to the findings, and through them to the routine.

Agreement is not always a commission

An accept may mint nothing — the problem is handled elsewhere, by hand, in a decision no work item would honestly capture. Minting stays the default and refusing it is the deliberate act, because the failure modes are asymmetric: a spurious backlog item is visible and deletable, a silent commission is not findable at all.

The surface says which happened rather than showing an accepted proposal with a blank item beside it, and an uncommissioned acceptance makes no claim on the routine's outflow.

Accept does not promote

The item lands in the backlog, rankable, behind the promote gate that already exists. Acceptance is a judgment about the proposal, not a decision to start work now — and conflating them would put a fleet's opinion straight into flight.

Findings are linked, never pasted

The case is prose a person can decide on without re-reading the evidence; the evidence is a table below it that stays live — the same rows sheet 2 triages, one entity across two surfaces.

Deciding a proposal moves none of them. Accepting shows the work item beside a finding and leaves it open: a decision about a proposal is never an observation about the ground.

The class is the query, not decoration

The hub never reads prevent, remediate, or mechanize. It groups by them — which is what makes “this routine's accepted proposals are all remediation, so it is being weeded while its source runs untouched” a fact rather than a feeling.

A passed proposal is evidence too

The mechanize proposal on this docket was passed with a reason: the judgment is genuinely ambiguous and stays at the LLM rung. That is the record that stops the same lint rule being proposed every quarter — and it is why passing needs a note more than accepting does.

Open — the campaign shape

A remediate proposal over forty findings is one work item, not forty. The accept dialog offers a campaign-fanout graph as the alternative, but whether one accepted proposal may mint a fan-out of cheap per-finding chunks — and how their outflow attributes back — is not settled here.

Open — superseding a proposal

Two runs weeks apart can propose the same response. Cross-referencing is supposed to catch it, but nothing on this docket lets a person say “this one supersedes that one” — today the answer is to pass the older with a note, which works and reads badly.
